# Employment and Labour Basics

Most private-sector employees in the UAE work under a written employment contract that sets out the job title, salary, working hours, and leave. The standard arrangement is a fixed-term contract, and both sides must give notice before ending it. Employees are generally entitled to annual paid leave, sick leave, and public holidays, along with an end-of-service benefit that is calculated from their basic salary and length of service. Working hours are capped by law, with extra pay or time off for overtime in many cases. Wages are commonly paid through an approved electronic salary system so that payments are tracked and protected. If a disagreement arises, employees can raise a complaint with the relevant labour authority, which offers mediation before a case moves to the courts.
