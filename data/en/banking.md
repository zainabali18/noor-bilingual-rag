# Banking and Everyday Finance

Residents in the UAE can open a current or savings account once they have a residence visa and an Emirates ID, and banks usually ask for a salary certificate or proof of income. The banking system is modern, with strong mobile apps, instant local transfers, and widely accepted debit and credit cards. Newcomers often start with a basic salary account and later add savings or investment products. Many banks offer accounts tailored to different income levels, and some have minimum-balance requirements that waive monthly fees. International transfers are common because many residents send money abroad, and licensed exchange houses offer competitive rates alongside the banks. It is wise to compare fees, foreign-exchange spreads, and card benefits before choosing a provider, since these vary noticeably between banks.
