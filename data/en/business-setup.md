# Starting a Business and Free Zones

People who want to start a company in the UAE usually choose between a mainland licence and a free-zone licence. A mainland company can trade freely across the local market and bid for government work, while a free zone offers a self-contained environment, simpler setup, and full foreign ownership in most activities. Each free zone focuses on certain sectors, such as technology, media, finance, or logistics, and provides office space, visas, and business services in one place. The typical steps are choosing the business activity, reserving a trade name, applying for the licence, arranging premises, and then processing residence visas for the owners and staff. Costs vary widely depending on the activity, the number of visas, and whether physical office space is required.
