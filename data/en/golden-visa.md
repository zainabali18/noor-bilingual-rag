# UAE Golden Visa

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence permit that lets qualified people live, work, and study in the country without needing a local sponsor. It is typically granted for five or ten years and renews automatically as long as the holder keeps meeting the criteria. Common eligibility routes include investors, entrepreneurs, highly skilled professionals, outstanding students and graduates, and people with specialised talent in fields such as science, technology, medicine, and the arts. A key advantage over standard employment visas is that holders can stay in the country for longer periods while outside the UAE, and they can sponsor their family members. Applicants usually submit proof of their qualification route, a valid passport, and supporting documents through the official government channels or an approved service centre.
