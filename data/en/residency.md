# Residency and Entry Visas

Visitors to the UAE may enter visa-free, with a visa on arrival, or with a visa arranged in advance, depending on their nationality. Those who want to stay longer move onto a residence visa, which is most often sponsored by an employer, a family member, a property investment, or a long-term scheme such as the Golden Visa. A residence visa is usually tied to an Emirates ID, the national identity card that residents use for most official services. Holders must complete a medical check and biometric registration as part of the process. Residence visas have validity periods and need to be renewed before they expire, and letting one lapse can lead to fines. Residents can also sponsor eligible family members once they meet the income and housing requirements.
