# Cost of Living

The cost of living in the UAE depends heavily on lifestyle and which emirate you live in. Housing is usually the largest expense, and rents in central parts of Dubai and Abu Dhabi are higher than in the smaller emirates. There is no personal income tax on salaries, which is a major draw, though a value-added tax applies to most goods and services. Day-to-day costs such as groceries, transport, and utilities are moderate, while international schooling and private healthcare can add up for families. Many residents budget carefully for annual rent, which is often paid in a small number of cheques, and for one-off costs like visa renewals and car registration. Public transport in the larger cities is affordable and reliable, which helps reduce the need for a private car.
