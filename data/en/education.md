# Education and Schooling

The UAE offers a wide choice of schools, including international schools that follow British, American, International Baccalaureate, Indian, and other curricula, alongside public schools. Because of this variety, many families choose a school based on the curriculum that best matches their plans for higher education. Private schools charge tuition fees that vary by curriculum, grade, and reputation, and places at popular schools can be limited, so parents often apply well ahead of the school year. Schools are regulated and inspected, and published ratings help families compare quality. Higher education is also strong, with local universities and international branch campuses offering degrees across many fields. Scholarships and university partnerships are available, and students graduating from UAE institutions can often move onto work or long-term residence routes.
