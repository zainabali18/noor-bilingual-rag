# Healthcare System

The UAE has a mix of public and private healthcare, and the standard of hospitals and clinics is generally high, especially in the larger cities. Health insurance is mandatory in several emirates, and employers are often required to provide cover for their staff. The scope of a plan decides which hospitals, clinics, and treatments are included, so residents should check their network and any co-payment before seeking care. Pharmacies are widespread and many operate long hours, while emergency services can be reached through a national hotline. Routine care such as general check-ups, dentistry, and specialist consultations is readily available, though costs without suitable insurance can be high. Families usually choose a plan that covers maternity and children's care, and it is common to compare providers during the annual renewal.
